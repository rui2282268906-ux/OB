Database buyers do not only ask “is it powerful?” They ask “how risky is migration?” This flow gives architects a concrete path from evaluation to cutover.

1

Assess

2

3

Validate

4

Cut over

Move traffic gradually with minimal downtime and measurable operational checkpoints.

5

Scale
